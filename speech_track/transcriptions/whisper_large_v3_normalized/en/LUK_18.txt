and he cried out jesus son of david have mercy on me and those who were in front rebuked him telling him but he cried out all the more son of david have mercy on me and jesus stopped and commanded him to be brought to him and when he came near he asked him what do you want me to do for you he said lord let me recover my sight and jesus said to him recover your sight your faith has made you well and
